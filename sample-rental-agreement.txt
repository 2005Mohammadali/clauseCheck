RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This agreement is made between Mr. R. Deshmukh ("Landlord") and the person signing below ("Tenant") for the flat at Baner, Pune.

1. Term. The tenancy is for 11 months with a lock-in period of 11 months. If the Tenant leaves before the lock-in ends for any reason, the Tenant must pay the full rent for all remaining months immediately.

2. Rent and Increase. Monthly rent is Rs. 28,000, payable by the 1st of every month. The Landlord may increase the rent at any time at his sole discretion by giving 7 days written notice, and the Tenant agrees to pay the revised rent.

3. Security Deposit. The Tenant shall pay a refundable security deposit of Rs. 3,00,000 (ten months rent). The deposit will be returned within 90 days after the Tenant vacates, after deductions that the Landlord alone shall determine for repairs, painting, and any other charges he considers appropriate.

4. Late Payment. If rent is delayed beyond the 3rd of the month, the Tenant shall pay a penalty of Rs. 500 per day until paid.

5. Landlord Entry. The Landlord or his representatives may enter the flat at any time, with or without notice, for inspection or any other purpose.

6. Termination. The Landlord may terminate this agreement at any time by giving 15 days notice. The Tenant may terminate only after the lock-in period and must give 3 months notice.

7. Maintenance. The Tenant shall bear all repairs including structural and plumbing repairs regardless of cause, and the Landlord shall not be liable for any damage, loss, or injury suffered by the Tenant in the flat.

8. Disputes. All disputes shall be resolved by arbitration by an arbitrator appointed solely by the Landlord, and the Tenant waives the right to approach any court.

9. Utilities. Electricity, water, and society maintenance charges are to be paid by the Tenant as per the bills.

Signed by both parties on the date written above.
